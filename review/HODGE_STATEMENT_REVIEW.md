## 1. Verdict

**Faithful with caveats.** The Lean statement at `CH/Challenge.lean:38-43`, with its hypotheses unfolded through `CH/CM.lean` and the FW definitions, is the paper's Theorem 1.1 (paper :25-29): for every complex abelian variety with complex multiplication (point included) and every `p ≥ 0`, every rational Hodge class in `H^{2p}` is a rational combination of fundamental classes of codimension-`p` integral subvarieties. No clause is weaker than the headline, no hypothesis is unsatisfiable or trivially satisfied by non-CM varieties, and no definition was found degenerate in either direction (Hodge-class space too small, cycle span too large). The caveats are: (a) the CM hypothesis is stated in the paper's Lemma 2.3(ii) form (rank-one lines over CM fields acting by Hodge endomorphisms), not the displayed definition (iii) (`End(A)⊗ℚ` contains a commutative semisimple algebra of dimension `2 dim A`); the equivalence is a paper-level fact (paper :279-283), not a Lean theorem; (b) the holomorphic-forms sheaf behind `F^p` is built by an unusual route (Kähler forms of the ring of holomorphic functions modulo a chart-evaluation kernel, `FW/Definitions/AlgebraicGeometry/Hodge/AnalyticDifferentialForms.lean:822-829`); the tree proves the lemmas that identify it with the standard sheaf, but no single theorem states "this is the Hodge filtration"; (c) no object satisfying all four instance hypotheses is constructed in either tree, so non-vacuity is mathematical, not formally witnessed; (d) the statement framework (257 files) and the lakefile that wires it in were written and, on 29 Oct 2026, rewritten by the solver, so the Comparator trust assumption "challenge closure controlled by you or trustworthy" is met only by reading it, which is what this review does.

Because the statement is faithful and the headline is an open conjecture whose truth implies (via Milne) the Tate conjecture for abelian varieties over finite fields and the Hodge standard conjecture for abelian varieties (paper Section 8, :2624-2640, :2770-2831), a Comparator PASS would mean that the weight of scrutiny moves to the ~300k-line proof tree and to the deeper FW definitions (cohomology, holomorphic forms, fundamental class), not to the statement.

## 3. Confirmed findings

### 3.1 CM hypothesis is Lemma 2.3(ii), not the displayed definition (iii)
Severity: minor.
Claim: `CMH1Data` (`CH/CM.lean:41-58`) encodes CM as "`H^1(X;ℚ)` is a product of rank-one modules over CM fields whose diagonal action preserves `F^1` under `h1Comparison`". The paper's displayed definition (paper :21-22) is "`End(A)⊗ℚ` contains a commutative semisimple ℚ-algebra of dimension `2 dim A`"; Lemma 2.3 (:279-283) proves (ii) ⇔ (iii), and the paper's proof of Proposition 2.6 consumes form (ii) (:421). `docs/032.md` paragraph 3 describes the hypothesis in the (ii) form.
Evidence: `CH/CM.lean:33-36` types `h1Comparison` only as a ℚ-linear map; `:48-53` states `F^1`-preservation one-directionally for every `a`. No lemma in FW states bijectivity of the comparison (grep of FW for `fieldToDeRham` with `Bijective|Injective|Surjective` is empty). The solution tree proves it unconditionally for smooth projective `X`: `OAI/ComplexMultiplication/BareComparison.lean:83-98` `h1Comparison_bijective`, `h1ComparisonLinearEquiv`, and `:97-98` `restrictScalars ℚ = Definitions.h1Comparison X := rfl`; `OAI/ComplexMultiplication/Hodge/Endomorphisms.lean:30-48` formalises "preserves `F^1`" as the Hodge-endomorphism condition.
Impact: none on the strength of the result. The direction needed for "Lean theorem ⇒ paper headline" is (iii) ⇒ (ii), which the paper proves by a dimension count without Riemann's theorem (:284-306). One-directional `F^1`-preservation suffices because the action is ℚ-rational and so commutes with conjugation. A report must say that the formal hypothesis is the rank-one-CM-lines form.
Verifiers' reasoning: three verifiers agreed on the facts; one voted to refute as cosmetic. Retained as a wording obligation, severity minor.

## 6. Buzzard's `IsProjective` question

The worry has a real basis in form and none in effect.
